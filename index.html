Help

Kosh says "Set a screen lock". Kosh's encryption key is protected by your phone's screen lock. Add a PIN, pattern or fingerprint in Android Settings → Security.

New phone. Make an encrypted backup in Kosh (Settings → Backup), move the file to the new phone, install Kosh and restore it with your passphrase.

Emergency info doesn't show on the lock screen. Fill in your blood group, allergies or an emergency contact under Settings → your profile, and allow Kosh's notifications. Some phones also need "Show all notification content" on the lock screen.
