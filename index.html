What it does

Scan onceKosh crops the card and reads the name, number and dates for you.
The right card, right awayLicence and vehicle papers when you drive, health cards near a hospital.
Never miss a renewalReminders before documents, insurance and services expire.
For the whole familyCards and vehicles for everyone at home, with emergency info on the lock screen.
Share only what's neededStamped copies and masked numbers, so a copy can't be reused.
Private by designEncrypted behind your fingerprint or PIN. No cloud copy, no ads.

Help

Kosh says "Set a screen lock". Kosh's encryption key is protected by your phone's screen lock. Add a PIN, pattern or fingerprint in Android Settings → Security.

New phone. Make an encrypted backup in Kosh (Settings → Backup), move the file to the new phone, install Kosh and restore it with your passphrase.

Emergency info doesn't show on the lock screen. Fill in your blood group, allergies or an emergency contact under Settings → your profile, and allow Kosh's notifications. Some phones also need "Show all notification content" on the lock screen.

Anything else. Write to mail@nexivaservices.com.
